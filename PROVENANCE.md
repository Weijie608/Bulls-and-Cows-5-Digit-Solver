# Provenance and Prior Work

## Source of this publication

This publication is maintained by [Weijie608](https://github.com/Weijie608).
Its source is the hobby project
[Bulls-and-Cows](https://github.com/Weijie608/Bulls-and-Cows), specifically
[`guess5/optimized_20260925_052028/`](https://github.com/Weijie608/Bulls-and-Cows/tree/main/guess5/optimized_20260925_052028).

The three original strategy and result files are retained without modification.
Their exact byte hashes are in the published `summary.json` and `SHA256SUMS`.
The date-like snapshot name identifies the retained development result; it is
not the date of publication of this repository.

The development repository retains a sequence of strategies with maximum
success count 7 and progressively smaller totals. This publication contains
the retained strategy with total 171,689. The original independent
`verify_strategy_db.py` is also retained without modification.

`CONCEPTS.md` is adapted from the development project's concepts document.
This publication adds format documentation, a decoder and independent replay
of the Base85 export, a complete explicit success tree, machine-readable
statistics, consistency checks, and automated verification.

## What can be reproduced

All published performance statistics can be recalculated from the stored
strategy. The database and Base85 export are compared decision by decision.
The complete tree, summary and checksums can be regenerated byte-for-byte.

The historical optimization programs that produced the selected snapshot are
not included in the retained development directory. This publication therefore
does not offer a complete rerun of that search. The `.strategy.b85` file is a
serialized set of decisions, not the optimizer's source code.

The verification establishes that this particular strategy works on every
secret. It does not rule out better strategies, establish optimality of every
continuation, or define a policy on every arbitrary subset of the universe.

## Related results and limits on novelty claims

- **Michael Hürter, 2013:** an
  [alt.math.recreational discussion](https://groups.google.com/g/alt.math.recreational/c/2AVMIplO9dY)
  reports seven moves or fewer for five pegs and ten digits with 30,240
  possibilities. The discussion title concerns another Mastermind variant;
  the five-digit Bulls-and-Cows statement is in its body. A matching downloadable
  strategy and an explicit stopping convention were not established in our
  review, so this publication does not claim priority for the number seven.
- **Chao-Lin Liu and Yu-Sheng Chen, 2012:**
  [Toward the optimum performance in the Bulls-and-Cows games](https://ah.lib.nccu.edu.tw/item?item_id=110905&locale=en)
  reports a five-digit mean of 5.7800 in its abstract. The available abstract
  alone does not establish all the rule, sampling and counting details needed
  for a direct ranking against this exhaustive result.
- **Kenji Tanaka:** the published
  [MOO strategies and tables](https://www.tanaka.ecc.u-tokyo.ac.jp/ktanaka/moo/moo-en.html)
  concern **four digits**, not the five-digit universe used here.

These references provide context, not a comprehensive survey or a claim that
this is the first public five-digit strategy. The publication makes no world
record claim. Its concrete contribution is an explicit, inspectable strategy
with independently checkable results for precisely stated rules.

## Separate game projects

- [guess5-web](https://github.com/Weijie608/guess5-web): the five-digit
  human-guessing game, available at
  [its live page](https://weijie608.github.io/guess5-web/).
- [guess5-computer-web](https://github.com/Weijie608/guess5-computer-web): the
  five-digit computer-guessing game, available at
  [its live page](https://weijie608.github.io/guess5-computer-web/). It uses the
  strategy published here and reveals the secret immediately upon identification.

Both games' interface code is maintained in their separate repositories and is
not bundled with this results publication.
